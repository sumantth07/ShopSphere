# Shopping, cart and checkout

## Finding products
The Shop page supports search by product name or brand, category, brand, price range, minimum rating, in-stock filter and sorting, with server-side pagination. Filters are kept in the page URL so you can share a filtered view.

## Wishlist
Signed-in customers can save products to a wishlist from product cards and product pages, and move items from the wishlist to the cart.

## Cart
The cart is stored on the server for signed-in users. You cannot add more than the available stock. Prices and totals are recalculated by the server every time. If an item becomes unavailable or its stock drops, the cart shows a notice.

## Checkout
Choose or add a delivery address, review the order and pick a payment method. Currently the only method is Cash on Delivery (demo). Stock is checked again when you place the order; if an item sold out, nothing is ordered and your cart stays intact. One checkout can contain products from several sellers.

## Comparing products
Select two to four products with the Compare control and open the Compare page for a side-by-side table of price, brand, category, rating, review count, stock and seller. Missing data is shown as unavailable.
