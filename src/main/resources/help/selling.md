# Selling on ShopSphere

## Becoming a seller
Signed-in customers open Become a Seller and submit a store name, description, contact email and optional logo URL and phone. The application starts as Pending. An administrator approves or rejects it and you get a notification. Only approved sellers can open the seller dashboard. A rejected applicant can resubmit. The same login is used for buying and selling.

## Seller dashboard
Approved sellers can manage their own products (create, edit, deactivate, images, stock), see low-stock and out-of-stock alerts, view and advance their own shipments, and see insights: active products, total stock, orders by status, revenue, trends, best sellers and cancellations. Sellers can never see another seller's products or orders.

## Stock
Every product has a stock quantity. Customers cannot buy more than is in stock, stock is reduced when an order is placed and restored once when eligible items are cancelled. Stock for products imported from the starting catalog is demo data.

## Revenue figures
Revenue is the purchase-time price times quantity of items that are not cancelled, excluding shipping. Delivered revenue counts only delivered shipments. There is no commission calculation.

## Suspension
An administrator can suspend a seller. Suspended sellers cannot manage anything and their products are hidden from the shop.
