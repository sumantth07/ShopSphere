# About ShopSphere

## What it is
ShopSphere is a portfolio project: a multi-seller e-commerce marketplace with a searchable catalog imported from a CSV file, customer accounts, seller accounts, an admin dashboard, simulated delivery tracking, and this AI shopping assistant.

## What is demo data
Imported product stock quantities, delivery tracking and payments are demonstration data. No real money moves and nothing is really shipped.

## What the assistant can and cannot do
The assistant answers from the live catalog and these help pages. It can search, recommend, compare and explain features, and, when you are signed in, explain your own orders. It cannot place or cancel orders, change prices or stock, or see other customers' information. Responses are AI generated and can be wrong, so check details on the product page.
